# Example plans

Copy one next to your video (in its `.davnai` folder), change the file names, and let your AI fill in the transcript line numbers after `python davnai.py transcribe`. The `"_about"` line is only a note.

| example | what it makes |
|---|---|
| [talking-head-short.json](talking-head-short.json) | A punchy Short from a talking-head recording: hook first, big captions, zooms, a name tag and an end screen. |
| [podcast-clip.json](podcast-clip.json) | A podcast clip from a wide two-person shot: whole frame on a blurred background, big centred captions, a cleaner voice. |
| [faceless.json](faceless.json) | A faceless video: a voice-over (yours, a free AI voice or your Voicebox clone) over stock clips and pictures, timed by its transcript. |
| [youtube-edit.json](youtube-edit.json) | A full YouTube edit: mistakes and dead air removed, intro and outro from the Brand Kit, chapter cards and a logo. |
| [split-gameplay.json](split-gameplay.json) | Talking on top, gameplay (or any B-roll) underneath, captions on the middle line. |
| [words-behind.json](words-behind.json) | Big words behind you on the key line, a beat pulse under music, and a warm light leak to open. |

Everything you can add: [library/](../library/). How plans work: [SKILL.md](../skills/davnai-video/SKILL.md#plan-format).
