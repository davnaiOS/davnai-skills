# Caption animations

- **pop**: Grows in.
- **bounce**: Drops and bounces.
- **fade**: Fades in.
- **slide**: Tips forward into place.
- **type**: Word by word.
- **none**: Appears.

```json
"captions": true,
"caption_animation": "pop",
"emphasis": ["free", "mistake"]
```

Captions use the Brand Kit's caption style or the theme's, and keep clear of the app buttons on tall videos.
