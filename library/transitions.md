# Transitions

- **fade**: Dips to black.
- **flash**: A white flash.
- **dissolve**: One shot melts into the next.
- **whip**: A fast smooth slide.
- **slide**: Slides left.
- **slide_up**: Slides up.
- **zoom**: Zooms into the next shot.
- **wipe**: A straight wipe.
- **circle**: Opens in a circle.
- **glitch**: A pixel glitch.
- **blur**: A horizontal blur.

```json
"transition": "flash"
```

Themes pick one for you; the plan can change it. Talking videos: cut or flash. Faceless: slide or dissolve.
