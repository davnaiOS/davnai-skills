# Scene

Your video moves into a card, a small window or the lower half over a designed background (grid, gradient, dark, paper, dots, a colour or a picture), with a headline, a list or a counting number, then back to full screen. The main tool for making a talking video feel designed.

**In the plan** (inside `"motion": [...]`):

```json
{ "type": "scene", "layout": "window", "background": "grid", "kicker": "The setup", "title": "$7,000 a year for 30 years", "highlight": ["$7,000"], "lines": [4, 5] }
```
