# Feed scroll

Your video plays inside a social post that scrolls up a phone feed into place (with your handle, a like and a caption), then zooms back to full screen.

**In the plan** (inside `"motion": [...]`):

```json
{ "type": "feed", "caption": "this changed everything", "likes": "12.4K", "lines": [5, 6] }
```
