# Colour looks

- **warm**: Golden, friendly.
- **cool**: Blue, clean.
- **vivid**: Rich colour.
- **punchy**: Contrast and sharpness.
- **bw**: Black and white.
- **cinematic**: Film colour with a vignette.
- **soft**: Gentle and bright.
- **vintage**: Faded film.
- **retro**: Faded film with grain.
