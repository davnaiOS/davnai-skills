# Davnai hooks

The first second decides whether anyone watches. A hook makes one specific person think "that's me" or "wait, what?".

## How to write one

1. Know who it's for and the one payoff of the video or post.
2. Pick 3 formulas from `reference/hooks.md` that fit the payoff (not the same family twice).
3. Fill them with their words and real specifics (numbers, names of things, a concrete situation). Specific beats clever.
4. Offer the 3, say which you'd use and why in one line, and let them choose.
5. Check: under 12 words if spoken; true (no fake claims); the video or post delivers it in the first few seconds; it sounds like them.

## On video

- Say the hook in the first second, before any intro or logo. Put the same words on screen (`hook_text` in the davnai-video plan) for people watching without sound.
- If the strongest line comes later in the recording, start the Short there (the plan's `keep` can put it first).

## Avoid

Clickbait the video doesn't pay off, "you won't believe…", shaming the viewer, copying another creator's exact line, and starting with "Hey guys, today…".
