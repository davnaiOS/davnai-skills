# Clips and pictures: cheapest source first

1. **The person's own files.** Ask where their B-roll or photos are. Their own material always comes first.
2. **Free stock sites** (for example Pexels or Pixabay). Download with the site's own download links, keep a note of each file's page, and follow the site's current licence (some ask for a credit line; never imply the people shown endorse anything). Prefer clips at least as large as the output (1080×1920 for tall videos).
3. **Pictures your own app can make**, or simple graphics drawn in code (title cards, charts).
4. **Davnai's make_picture** only when the person asks for Davnai's. It uses their Davnai plan's picture allowance.

Never use clips from films, TV, music videos or other creators' posts.
